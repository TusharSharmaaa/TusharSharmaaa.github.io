Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026
Applies to: all games published by BIGFUR Studio on Google Play, including Grow Blocks: Zen Block Puzzle (package com.bigfurstudio.growblocks) and Block Rise: Block Puzzle Game (package com.blockrise.stackrush).

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and our games have no accounts.
  • Your progress (scores, flowers, settings, your current game) is stored only on your device. We never receive it.
  • Our games are free and show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the app keeps a small file on your phone with your best score, flowers collected, game settings (sound, music, vibration) and, if you leave mid-game, your current board. It also keeps simple ad-timing counters (for example, when an ad was last shown) so that we can limit how often ads appear. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to BIGFUR Studio. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show ads. When ads are requested, Google and its partners may automatically collect:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address (which can indicate approximate location);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • ad interactions and performance data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped), plus diagnostics used to prevent fraud and to measure ads.

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent tool). You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On any Android device you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device).
  • All ads in our games are optional to watch, except a short ad that may appear between games, never during play. Ad content is limited to the PG rating.

3. Google Play services

Our games are distributed through Google Play. Google Play may check that the app was installed from Google Play (to protect against tampered copies) and may show Google's in-app rating dialog. These features are provided by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children

Our games are designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an ad, please contact us and we will help you reach Google.

5. Data sharing and selling

BIGFUR Studio does not collect, sell or share personal information. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled by Google as described in section 2.

6. Security

Game data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app storage. Ad requests made by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. To delete game data, uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy controls. You can contact us anytime with questions.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
