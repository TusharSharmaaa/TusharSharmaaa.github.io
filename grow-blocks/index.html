Grow Blocks

A calm block puzzle where some of your blocks are seeds, and they grow. When a growing leaf completes a line by itself, it's a Bloom Clear.

How it plays

Drag blocks onto the board and fill rows or columns to clear them. Every time your tray refills, every seed grows one leaf in the direction of its arrow. You always see where it will grow next, so you can plan with the garden. Every flower you bloom lives forever in your own garden.
